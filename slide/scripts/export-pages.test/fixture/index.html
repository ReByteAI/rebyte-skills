Overview

Option 1: Mountain Lodge

A secluded resort in the Rockies. High altitude, crisp air, and zero distractions. Perfect for deep focus and hiking.

Option 2: Beachside Villa

Coastal property with ocean views. Relaxed vibe, outdoor workshops, and team surfing lessons in the afternoon.

Option 3: City Hub

Boutique hotel in downtown. Fast Wi-Fi, premium dining, and easy access to local tech meetups and nightlife.

2

Location Trade-off

Option 1: Mountain Lodge

Pros:
  • Incredible scenery and focus
  • Great for group hiking
  • Very private environment
Cons:
  • Longer travel time from HQ
  • Spotty cell service

Option 2: Beachside Villa

Pros:
  • High team morale and relaxation
  • Many outdoor team activities
  • Shorter flight for most
Cons:
  • More expensive during peak season
  • Potential for distraction

Option 3: City Hub

Pros:
  • Easiest logistics and travel
  • Best internet and meeting spaces
  • Endless dining options
Cons:
  • Lacks the "getaway" feeling
  • Harder to keep team together
