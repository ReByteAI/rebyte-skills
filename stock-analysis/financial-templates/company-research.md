# Company Research Template

**Template status:** Use this to structure a company memo. Fill with cited
filings, provider data, news, transcripts, or user-supplied materials.

## Header

- Company: `<name / ticker>`
- Date: `<YYYY-MM-DD>`
- Scope: `<investment memo, credit review, competitor profile, etc.>`
- Sources used: `<list with dates>`

## Memo Structure

### 1. Business Snapshot

- What the company sells.
- Customer segments and geographies.
- Revenue model.
- Segment mix and margin profile.

### 2. Financial Profile

- Revenue growth and drivers.
- Margin trend and operating leverage.
- Cash flow conversion.
- Balance sheet and liquidity.

### 3. Competitive Position

- Market share or relative scale.
- Differentiation.
- Switching costs / barriers to entry.
- Key competitors.

### 4. Catalysts

- Earnings, product launches, regulatory decisions, capital allocation.
- Timing and measurable indicators.

### 5. Risks

- Demand, margin, execution, leverage, regulation, governance.
- Separate known risks from inferred risks.

### 6. Open Questions

- Missing data.
- Claims requiring source verification.
- Follow-up analysis needed.

## Output Pattern

Start with a 5-bullet executive read, then support each bullet with sourced
evidence. Use exact dates and units for financial claims.
